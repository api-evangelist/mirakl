Mirakl is the global leader in platform business innovation, providing an operating system for commerce that lets retailers, brands, and B2B distributors launch and scale online marketplaces and dropship programs without holding inventory. The platform spans the Mirakl Marketplace Platform (MMP), Mirakl Platform for Services (MPS), the Mirakl Catalog Platform, Mirakl Connect for multichannel selling, Mirakl Ads retail media, and Mirakl Payout. Mirakl exposes extensive REST APIs (OpenAPI 3.1) for sellers/shops and operators covering orders, offers, products, catalog, invoicing, messaging, returns, and shipments, plus a Connect Channel Platform with push webhooks for offer, price/stock, product, order-action, and store events.

**Areas:** Company · Commerce · E-Commerce · Marketplace · Dropship · Retail · Catalog · Order · Retail Media · B2B

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Mirakl: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
